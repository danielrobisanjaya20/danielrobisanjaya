ABOUT

Hi! I am DANIEL ROBI SANJAYA.

I created this website as a means of entertainment and personal archive. In this section, I will tell you a little about myself. I am the second child of three siblings. I was born in 2000. I really love education, so I strive to get the highest level of education possible. My most recent educational background is a master's degree from Diponegoro University, majoring in Information Systems and obtaining an M.Kom degree. Previously, I also obtained an S.Pd degree from IKIP PGRI Pontianak, which is now PGRI Pontianak University, majoring in Information Technology Education. During my undergraduate and graduate studies, I achieved CUM LAUDE honors at both levels. This achievement motivates me to continue developing and improving myself. Currently, I am working as a lecturer in the Management Study Program at Widya Dharma University in Pontianak, West Kalimantan. That's all for now, a little introduction about myself. Nice to meet you! Thank you.
